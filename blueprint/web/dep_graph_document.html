Definition 1Finite distribution

A distribution \(\mu \) on a finite set \(X\) is a weight \(\mu (x) \ge 0\) for each \(x \in X\), with \(\sum _x \mu (x) = 1\).

Definition 9Payoff

Definition 7Strategies

A pure strategy of player \(i\) is a report rule \(T_i \to M_i\) and an action rule \(T_i \times M_i \times R_i \to A_i\). A mixed strategy profile assigns each player a distribution over pure strategies.

Lemma 16Deviation outcome equivalence

Deviating to \(\rho \) under \(d^*\) has the same outcome as playing the mimic of \(\rho \) under \(d\).

Lemma 15Truthful outcome equivalence

Truthful play under \(d^*\) has the same outcome as \(\pi \) under \(d\).

Theorem 18Revelation Principle, existence form

If \(\pi \) is a communication equilibrium of \(G(d)\), some direct device has truthful play as a communication equilibrium with the same outcome.

Theorem 17Revelation Principle, canonical form

If \(\pi \) is a communication equilibrium of \(G(d)\), then truthful play is a communication equilibrium of \(G(d^*)\) with the same outcome.

Theorem 19Revelation Principle

\(\mu \) is a communication-equilibrium outcome if and only if \(\mu \) is the outcome of a truthful equilibrium under some direct device.
